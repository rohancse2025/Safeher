How volunteer alerts work

        
When you are available, SafeHer notifies you if an emergency SOS is triggered within 2 km of your location.

        
Always prioritize your safety: call police (112), alert people nearby, and do not put yourself in danger.

        
Note: Real-time alerting across different mobile phones requires backend cloud services (Web Push / WebSockets).
