SafeHer Privacy Policy

Plain English commitment to your privacy

inventory_2 1. What is collected

SafeHer collects only the information essential to protect your personal safety:

  • Account details: Your full name, 10-digit phone number, email address, and hashed password.
  • Emergency contacts: The names, phone numbers, and optional email addresses of the trusted people you designate.
  • GPS Location: Your real-time coordinates are collected only while an emergency SOS alert, silent SOS, or live tracking is active. SafeHer never tracks or logs your location in the background when no alert is running.
Important: Your location is strictly private by default and is only accessed when you explicitly trigger an alert.

help_outline 2. Why it is collected

All information is gathered strictly for emergency safety purposes:

  • To notify your designated emergency contacts immediately with your live GPS location during distress.
  • To alert verified nearby community volunteers within a 2 km radius who can respond quickly.
  • To allow trusted guardians to view your live tracking route through a temporary secure link until you mark yourself safe.

visibility 3. Who can see your information

  • Your emergency contacts: When you trigger an alert, your contacts receive an SMS/email with your location link.
  • Approved nearby volunteers: Approved volunteers within 2 km receive proximity notifications showing only the distance to your location. For silent SOS alerts, your name is never shown to volunteers.
  • Public tracking page: Anyone with your unique temporary tracking token can see only your first name, alert status, and current GPS pin. No phone number or email is ever exposed.

We never sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for commercial tracking.

schedule 4. How long it is kept

  • GPS location points: All GPS location trail records are automatically and permanently deleted after 30 days.
  • Account & contacts: Stored securely while your account is active.
  • Immediate deletion on demand: You can completely delete your account and all associated records (contacts, alerts, location points, volunteer profile, and timers) at any time from the Profile screen by selecting "Delete my account and data".
